# Google OAuth Configuration Guide

To enable "Sign in with Google" in your application, you need to configure the correct URLs in the Google Cloud Console. 

Google requires two specific types of URLs to ensure your authentication flow is secure:

## 1. Authorized JavaScript Origins
This is the URL of your **Frontend** application. It tells Google that requests coming from this domain are authorized to initiate the login process.

### Local Development (Testing)
- `http://localhost:3000`

### Production (Live App)
- `https://your-frontend-domain.com` *(Replace with your actual Vercel/live frontend URL)*

---

## 2. Authorized Redirect URIs
This is the URL of your **Backend** application where Google will send the user *after* they successfully sign in. Our backend Passport.js logic is specifically listening for this route to process the Google profile data.

### Local Development (Testing)
- `http://localhost:5000/api/auth/google/callback`

### Production (Live App)
- `https://your-backend-domain.com/api/auth/google/callback` *(Replace with your actual Render/live backend URL)*

> **Important:** The redirect URI in Google Console **must perfectly match** the URL generated by your backend. If you missed a trailing slash or misspelled `/api/auth/google/callback`, Google will throw a `redirect_uri_mismatch` error when you try to log in.

## Where to add these in Google Cloud Console:
1. Go to the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/).
2. Navigate to **APIs & Services** > **Credentials**.
3. Under "OAuth 2.0 Client IDs", click on your Web client (or create a new one).
4. Scroll down to the **Authorized JavaScript origins** section and click **+ ADD URI**. Enter your frontend URL(s).
5. Scroll down to the **Authorized redirect URIs** section and click **+ ADD URI**. Enter your backend callback URL(s).
6. Click **Save**.

> Keep in mind that changes in the Google Cloud Console can sometimes take up to 5-10 minutes to propagate across their servers.
